## YOUR KNOWLEDGE BASE

### FOUNDATION: DAILY BIAS (MECHANICAL FRAMEWORK)

Daily bias is the first thing determined before any trade. It answers: "Is price more likely to continue or reverse today?"

**Bias is BULLISH when:**
- Previous day closed above its PDH (Previous Day High) → target is above
- Price trades into PDL but forms a small wick and CISD → reversal long

**Bias is BEARISH when:**
- Previous day closed below its PDL → target is below
- Price trades into PDH but forms a small wick and CISD → reversal short

**Bias is NEUTRAL / Seek & Destroy when:**
- High-impact news approaching (CPI, FOMC, NFP)
- No HTF directional structure; daily in indecision after strong expansion
- London sweeps BOTH Asia highs and lows → range-bound day, avoid directional trades

**Critical rules:**
- A HTF bias is ALWAYS required. Never trade LTF setups against the HTF direction.
- Small wick = expansion potential. Large wick = limited range, target is close (open, session mid).
- CISD (Change in State of Delivery) = price closes above a series of down-close candles (bullish) or below a series of up-close candles (bearish). This confirms delivery direction.

### WEEKLY PROFILES (FOR BIAS CONTEXT)

Used to understand what the week is likely to do before drilling into daily bias.

**1. Classic Expansion (Trend Week)**
- Price expands in one direction all week.
- Monday establishes directional intent; Tuesday–Friday continue.
- Trade: continuation entries on pullbacks to OBs/FVGs.

**2. Consolidation Reversal (TGIF / Thursday Counter)**
- Monday–Wednesday: tight consolidation, no clear direction.
- Thursday: manipulates a range extreme (takes highs or lows), closes back INSIDE the range → traps traders.
- Friday: real expansion in the opposite direction of Thursday's sweep.
- Bearish version: Thursday takes Monday's high, closes below → Friday expands down.
- Bullish version: Thursday takes Monday's low, closes above → Friday expands up.
- Entry: aggressive on Thursday reversal candle, conservative waiting for Friday open.

**3. Intraweek Reversal (Midweek Reversal)**
- Early week expands in one direction.
- Wednesday or Thursday: reversal candle at a key HTF PD array (OB, FVG, HTF liquidity).
- Late week continues in the new direction.
- Confirmation: daily reversal candle close at a POI + hourly CISD.
- Key: "follow logic, not patterns." Not every midweek reversal is clean — skip if unclear.

**4. Seek & Destroy Week**
- No directional move forms. Price oscillates around weekly open all week.
- Usually pre-news or post-strong-expansion consolidation.
- Strategy: fade extremes back to equilibrium, reduce size, prioritize capital preservation.

### DAILY PROFILES (SESSION BEHAVIOR)

Once bias is set, determine which daily profile is playing out to know WHERE and WHEN to enter.

**1. London Reversal + New York Continuation (Most Common)**
- London session (2–5 AM EST): manipulates price in the OPPOSITE direction of daily bias, sweeps a liquidity pool, forms the daily WICK.
- CISD confirms during London → signals the real direction.
- New York AM (8:30–12:00 EST): drives the BODY of the daily candle in the direction of bias.
- Entry windows: 8:30, 9:30, or 10:00 AM EST on OBs/FVGs formed during London's reversal.
- Visual: "London = wick, New York = body."
- AVOID new entries in NY if London has already exhausted the day's range.

**2. New York Manipulation + Reversal**
- London: consolidates, no clear direction.
- New York (8:30 AM): sweeps the range extreme (false move, Judas Swing).
- NY then forms CISD and expands in the real direction.
- Entry: after CISD following NY sweep, using the OB/FVG created by the sweep.
- Key times: 8:30 AM (manipulation), 9:30 AM (continuation), 10:00 AM (secondary).

**3. New York Reversal**
- London: trades toward a key level but fails to reach the HTF PD array.
- New York AM: continues London's direction, hits the HTF key level, THEN shifts structure.
- This is the profile where the real reversal happens in New York, not London.
- Confirmation: displacement + CISD on 15M/5M after NY hits the HTF level.
- Entry: after CISD close, stop below the sweep low (bullish) or above the sweep high (bearish).
- Targets: failure swings, session highs/lows, HTF liquidity.

**4. Seek & Destroy (Avoid / Fade)**
- Price opens and closes near the same level. London sweeps both Asia extremes.
- No clean expansion forms; NY chops inside the range.
- Strategy: mark 50% equilibrium. Fade false breakouts back to the middle. Do not chase directional moves.

### TTRADES FRACTAL MODEL (TTFM)

The TTFM is TTrades' core mechanical system. It reads price as a sequence of candles, each with a specific role, applied fractally across all timeframes (daily→4H→1H→15M→5M→1M).

**Candle 1 — Reference / Swing Formation Candle**
- The prior swing high or low. It defines the level to be swept.
- It creates the liquidity pool that Candle 2 will target.

**Candle 2 — The Reversal Candle (The Setup)**
- Opens, makes a large opposing run (long wick), closes near its open.
- This is the liquidity sweep — it takes out Candle 1's high or low.
- Wick analysis is CRITICAL:
  - **Small wick on C2**: price hasn't used much range → expansion potential is HIGH. Can trade C2 directly.
  - **Large wick on C2**: price used most of its range creating the wick → expansion is LIMITED. Target only the open or session extremes. Wait for C3.
- Entry on C2 requires: CISD on the execution timeframe + confirmation from correlated asset (NQ vs ES SMT if applicable).
- **Timeframe for CISD by session:**
  - Asia: 1H or 30M
  - London: 1H, 30M, or 15M
  - New York: 15M (continuations), 5M (reversals)

**Candle 3 — The Continuation Candle**
- Trade C3 when C2 had a LARGE wick (limited C2 expansion).
- C2 closes, forming a protected high/low. C3 then expands in the confirmed direction.
- Confirmation signals on LTF: CISD + FVG + OB + SMT divergence (all aligning = highest probability).
- Skip C3 when C2 was a strong expansion candle → chasing the move is low probability.

**Candle 4 — Secondary Continuation (The Power Candle)**
- The 4th candle in a swing formation — one of the highest-probability setups.
- The market cannot reverse without forming a swing point. C4 is the continuation after that swing is confirmed.
- **Bullish C4 setup:**
  - C3 closes strongly bullish (confirmed swing low).
  - C4 expands out of the UPPER half of C3's range.
  - Wick on C4 forms in the upper half of C3's range.
- **Bearish C4 setup:**
  - C3 closes strongly bearish (confirmed swing high).
  - C4 expands out of the LOWER half of C3's range.
- **Three-timeframe execution:**
  1. Daily: identify swing, mark C3's 50% equilibrium.
  2. 30M/1H: find OBs, FVGs, structure within that range.
  3. 3–5M: confirm entry with CISD.
- SMT divergence between NQ and ES is strong confirmation for C4 pivots.
- Sessions: London often forms the C4 pivot; New York drives the expansion.

### ENTRY MODELS

**Unicorn Entry Model**
- Highest-probability entry: a **Breaker Block** overlapping with a **Fair Value Gap**.
- The overlap means institutional order flow and imbalance coincide → explosive moves.
- Best used during: Silver Bullet window (10:00–11:00 AM EST), any killzone with strong HTF alignment.
- Execution: enter at the overlap zone, stop beyond the Breaker Block, target the next liquidity pool.

**AM Silver Bullet (10:00–11:00 AM EST on NQ/ES)**
- Time-based strategy. Only trade within this 1-hour window.
- Setup: price grabs liquidity (BSL or SSL), returns into an FVG formed by that sweep.
- Entry: price trades back into FVG → drop to 1M for a secondary FVG → enter in the direction of the grab.
- Stop: beyond the liquidity sweep.
- Adjust stop to breakeven once trade hits 3R.
- All entries must be placed BEFORE 11:00 AM EST.

**OTE (Optimal Trade Entry)**
- Enter at the 61.8%–79% Fibonacci retracement of the C2 swing.
- Used after a confirmed CISD when price retraces into the OTE zone within a PD array (OB or FVG).

**1-Minute Inversion Entry**
- After a higher-TF entry is confirmed, drill to 1M to find an IFVG (Inversion Fair Value Gap).
- The IFVG is a former bullish FVG that price traded through — now acts as resistance (or vice versa for bearish).
- Entry at the 1M IFVG tightens risk dramatically.

**Protected Swing Entry**
- After CISD, the high or low formed by C2 becomes a "protected swing."
- Price must not close beyond this swing on the execution TF — if it does, setup is invalidated.
- Stop loss always goes beyond the protected swing.

### MARKET STRUCTURE TOOLKIT

**BOS (Break of Structure)** — continuation signal; confirms trend.
**CHoCH (Change of Character)** — first BOS against trend; potential reversal, requires LTF confirmation.
**MSS (Market Structure Shift)** — CHoCH on LTF that aligns with HTF intent; triggers execution.
**CISD (Change in State of Delivery)** — price closes above/below a series of opposing candles; confirms which direction price is being delivered. This is TTrades' primary confirmation tool.

**PD Arrays (in order of strength):**
1. Order Block (OB) — last opposing candle before displacement
2. Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG) — former FVG that price ran through; now flipped
3. Fair Value Gap (FVG) — 3-candle imbalance; price returns to fill
4. Breaker Block — failed OB that flipped polarity
5. Mitigation Block — return to the origin of an unfilled move
6. Equilibrium (50% of range) — used for continuations

**IRL → ERL Framework:**
- IRL (Internal Range Liquidity): FVGs, OBs, equilibrium within a range.
- ERL (External Range Liquidity): swing highs/lows, equal highs/lows outside the range.
- Price delivery goes: IRL → ERL → IRL → ERL. Know which one price is currently targeting.

**SMT Divergence (NQ vs ES):**
- NQ makes a new high but ES does not (or vice versa) = SMT bearish divergence → expect reversal.
- ES makes a new low but NQ does not = SMT bullish divergence → expect bounce.
- Used as confirmation at C2, C4, and Unicorn setups.

```
   → C4 setup (swing confirmed): highest conviction, execute 3-TF approach.

   → Breaker Block + FVG overlap → Unicorn Model.

6. CONFIRMATION CHECKLIST (must have before entry)

   ☑ Wick size assessed on C2 to set realistic targets
```

### RISK & TRADE MANAGEMENT

- **Stop loss**: always beyond the protected swing (the sweep low/high of C2).
- **Target**: next liquidity pool (opposing BSL/SSL), next HTF PD array, or StdDev projection (2.0–2.5 for conservative, 4.0–4.5 for extended).
- **Breakeven**: move stop to entry once trade hits 2R–3R.
- **Daily loss limit**: define max loss before session starts. If hit, STOP trading for the day.
- **Seek & Destroy conditions**: cut position size to minimum or skip entirely.
- **No entries**: last 5 minutes before close, first 5 minutes of RTH open (before structure forms).
- **Flatten**: 14:55 CT — no exceptions.
